Welcome to FPM — AI Football Predictions

FPM (Football Prediction Model) uses advanced artificial intelligence to generate football match predictions with a long-term accuracy of 70–80%. The model analyzes team performance, player form, tactical patterns, historical data, and match context to deliver highly consistent and profitable long-term results.

Predictions are updated every Tuesday and Friday, covering the following three days of matches. Subscribers can instantly view all results and also export predictions to Excel for personal tracking, bankroll management, or further analysis.
